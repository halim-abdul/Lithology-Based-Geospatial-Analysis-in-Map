# Research questions

The repository is structured to support progressively stronger analyses:

1. Do fault-kinematic proportions differ across lithologies?
2. Are strike orientations multimodal, and do modes vary by lithology?
3. Are steeply dipping planes spatially clustered?
4. Do localities with high observation counts dominate apparent regional trends?
5. How stable are lithology–kinematic associations under locality-level resampling?
6. Would projected-coordinate spatial statistics change conclusions suggested by simple longitude/latitude maps?

These questions can be extended with circular statistics, bootstrap uncertainty, and spatial point-process models.
