# Descriptive findings

These are dataset-level observations, not causal geological claims.

- The dataset contains **1,207 observations from 93 localities**.
- Geographic extent: **50.3131–51.4955°N** and **9.8600–12.0329°E**.
- Lithology is strongly imbalanced: Muschelkalk dominates with **1,013 observations**, followed by Granite (117), Buntsandstein (30), Zechstein (29), Basalt (13), and Rhyolite (5).
- Fault-slip classes are also uneven: normal (376), inverted (296), dextral (224), sinistral (153), extension (91), and pressure (67).
- Because sampling density and lithology are spatially structured, raw frequency should not be interpreted as regional prevalence without accounting for sampling design.

The analysis therefore reports both absolute counts and normalized within-lithology proportions.
